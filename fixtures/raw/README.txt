STAND-IN DATA, NOT A RESULT. Written by tools/make_standin_raw.py from a fixed seed.
The network in gtfs/ is real and public: nine routes of RTA's public timetable feed as published in late 2021 (opendata/public/rta-gtfs/PROVENANCE.md), with its calendar's dates moved to December 2025.
Everything else is invented: every passenger, tap, card, device, vehicle, driver, lateness and community here. None of it is RTA's data, and no number made from it is a result.
A real table from the hackathon must never be put in this folder. scripts/gates.sh proves these files equal a fresh build.
