# Insiyab imagery direction

## The city continues

Photographs should make movement feel composed, tangible and close to everyday life. The subject is a city being served well: a bus arriving cleanly at the kerb, a sheltered stop, a controller reviewing a decision. Intelligence remains in the background. Steel, glass, pale stone and the warm edge of daylight connect the imagery to the architectural character of the brief.

Use restrained documentary photography with straight verticals, plausible road geometry, accurate vehicle construction and natural optical depth. Keep the bus or stop legible as a real physical object. Preserve quiet space for an independently typeset headline. Do not ask the image model to render the identity, interface copy, numbers or Arabic lettering.

Ivory, muted metal, warm stone and deep evening neutrals should dominate. Natural photographic light may have tonal transitions; the prohibition on graphic gradients still applies to logos, backgrounds, charts and interface decoration. Champagne Gold is a material and light reference, not a metallic filter applied to the whole photograph.

All generated images are concept imagery. Do not present them as photographs of an actual RTA deployment, measured operational event or approved facility. Add captions outside the image when that distinction matters.

## Prompt 1 — City in flow

**Purpose:** Executive presentation opening or identity cover.

**Format:** 16:9 landscape, composed for 3840 × 2160 output. **Negative space:** Left 45 percent, with quiet pale architecture and uncluttered sky. **Camera:** 50 mm architectural photography; camera at pedestrian height. **Light:** Late afternoon golden hour, soft side lighting, dry clear air.

**Ready prompt**

> Photographic architectural editorial image of a calm Dubai avenue at late afternoon golden hour. One contemporary full-size low-floor city bus travels smoothly in the right half of the frame, seen in a believable three-quarter side view, with physically accurate wheels, windows, mirrors and right-side passenger doors. Its body is neutral ivory with restrained dark glazing and no operator branding. Pale stone paving, a real modern sheltered bus stop and slender steel-and-glass buildings establish a recognisable contemporary Dubai setting without turning the scene into a skyline postcard. Compose the bus and stop in the lower-right portion; reserve the entire left 45 percent for clean pale architectural surfaces and quiet sky that can hold a later headline. Straight vertical lines, natural perspective, 50 mm lens, refined documentary photography, controlled highlights, soft warm light, accurate neutral colours, fine realistic detail, premium but ordinary city life. No readable words, logos, route numbers, plates, government insignia or destination text. No identifiable faces; keep any distant pedestrians small and facing away. No neon, purple colour cast, graphic gradients, robots, glowing effects, holograms, AI symbols, extravagant motion trails, impossible vehicles, dramatic traffic jams, advertising copy or watermark. Landscape 16:9. Do not render any text into the image.

**Why it belongs:** The bus is the proof of usefulness; the architecture supplies stature. The open left side allows the message to lead without making the photograph compete with it.

## Prompt 2 — A place to arrive

**Purpose:** Section opener about passengers, stations or service continuity.

**Format:** 3:4 portrait. **Negative space:** Upper 35 percent, mainly quiet pale glass and sky. **Camera:** 70 mm lens at eye level; compressed but believable proportions. **Light:** Warm golden hour reflecting softly in glass.

**Ready prompt**

> A premium documentary photograph of a real-looking contemporary Dubai bus shelter at golden hour, portrait composition. Focus on the meeting of slender brushed-metal posts, clear glass panels, a clean bench and pale stone paving. A physically plausible full-size city bus approaches in the lower-right background, with neutral ivory bodywork and dark windows, accurate wheels and door placement, no operator livery or branding. The shelter should look usable and built, with realistic fixings, drainage and a clear accessible boarding area. Use a restrained 70 mm architectural lens and straight verticals. Keep the upper 35 percent calm and lightly toned, with mostly quiet sky or unmarked glass for a headline added later. Natural warm reflections, no tinted spectacle, fine detail, subtle material texture, gentle shadows, still and orderly atmosphere. Do not show readable signs, logos, schedules, destination displays, licence plates or text. No identifiable faces; ideally no people, or only one small rear-facing distant figure. No futuristic pods, autonomous-vehicle symbolism, robots, neon, purple, graphic overlays, sparkles, lens flare, excessive blur, dramatic clouds, AI motifs or watermark. Portrait 3:4. No typography in the image.

**Why it belongs:** A modest stop makes the brand relevant to public service. Precision comes from materials and framing, without turning the environment into a luxury advertisement.

## Prompt 3 — Coordination behind the scenes

**Purpose:** Slide about driver, depot and station coordination.

**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Negative space:** Left 40 percent, with one quiet service-bay wall. **Camera:** 85 mm lens from a safe elevated depot walkway. **Light:** Dusk, mixed natural blue-grey ambient and warm practical light.

**Ready prompt**

> Photographic editorial view of a plausible Dubai city-bus depot at early dusk from a safe elevated pedestrian walkway. Two contemporary full-size low-floor buses stand in orderly service bays on the right side of the frame, with realistic chassis proportions, mirrors, wheels, vents and door details. Their bodies are plain neutral ivory and dark glazing, with no logos, operator markings or readable text. Subtle warm practical lighting falls on the bus sides while cool natural dusk remains outside; keep colours quiet and believable. A slender steel canopy, clean bay edges and honest concrete surfaces create a precise working environment. Reserve the left 40 percent as a quiet pale service-bay wall and open space for editorial text added later. 85 mm lens, natural compression, straight geometry, realistic exposure, crisp but unaggressive detail. No readable signs, plates, bay numbers, routes or destination displays. No identifiable people or faces. No dramatic mechanical failure, sparks, sirens, crowds, theatrical fog, neon, purple cast, robots, floating data, glowing brains, holograms, branding or watermark. Calm premium documentary photography, landscape 16:9, no rendered typography.

**Why it belongs:** The frame values coordination and readiness. It gives the backstage work a credible presence while keeping the service itself central.

## Prompt 4 — The person makes the call

**Purpose:** Human control and decision responsibility.

**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Negative space:** Right 40 percent, formed by a quiet wall and soft window light. **Camera:** 50 mm lens, over-shoulder detail without a visible head or face. **Light:** Dusk with soft neutral practical lighting.

**Ready prompt**

> A carefully composed documentary photograph inside a plausible modern bus-operations room adjoining a Dubai depot at dusk. Show only the hands, forearms and lower rear shoulder of one human controller seated at a restrained desk in the left half of the frame; crop above the shoulders so no head, face, badge or identifiable personal detail is visible. One hand rests beside an ordinary keyboard and one hand is poised calmly over a control mouse, suggesting considered review. A monitor is present but its content is defocused and contains no readable text, logos, route labels or distinctive interface. Through a distant window, one real-looking full-size ivory city bus and a simple sheltered boarding point are visible, small and physically believable. Reserve the right 40 percent as quiet pale wall and gentle window light for a headline added later. 50 mm lens, natural perspective, soft neutral desk lighting balanced with cool dusk, warm stone and steel tones, tactile everyday materials, precise calm composition. No theatrical command centre, giant glowing maps, holograms, AI brains, robots, neon, purple, sci-fi devices, visible faces, readable data, insignia or watermark. Premium documentary photography, landscape 16:9, no generated lettering.

**Why it belongs:** A small human gesture communicates responsibility more clearly than a room of screens. The bus beyond the window keeps that responsibility connected to a physical service.

## Prompt 5 — The route as architecture

**Purpose:** A broad network or planning chapter opener.

**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Negative space:** Upper-left 45 percent, mainly quiet light stone and simple building planes. **Camera:** 70 mm lens from an elevated building viewpoint; no extreme drone distortion. **Light:** Low golden-hour sunlight.

**Ready prompt**

> An elevated architectural photograph of a calm contemporary Dubai street at golden hour, seen from a believable building viewpoint with a 70 mm lens rather than an extreme aerial angle. The road curves gently through the lower-right half of the composition and resolves into a straight run. One realistically constructed full-size low-floor city bus with neutral ivory panels and dark glazing follows the road near a real sheltered bus stop, fully grounded on the road with correct scale, wheels and shadows. Include pale paving, slender steel railings, glass facades and restrained roadside planting. Road markings should be physically plausible but no words or numbers are legible. The upper-left 45 percent should be composed of quiet pale architectural planes and open space suitable for text added later. Precise perspective, natural sunlight, clean highlights, soft long shadows, subtle atmosphere, no spectacle. No identifiable faces, readable logos, route signs, destination boards, licence plates, government emblems or building signage. No fantasy road interchanges, empty desert clichés, flying vehicles, geometric overlays, neon, purple, glow, data trails, robots or watermark. Photographic, premium, restrained, 16:9 landscape. Do not generate text.

**Why it belongs:** Real road geometry supplies the visual relationship to the symbol. The brand does not need to draw a network over a photograph to suggest flow.

## Prompt 6 — Quiet arrival at dusk

**Purpose:** Closing image, mobile background crop or service confirmation chapter.

**Format:** 9:16 portrait, retaining a usable 4:5 central crop. **Negative space:** Upper 40 percent, quiet glass and dusk sky. **Camera:** 50 mm lens at passenger height. **Light:** Early dusk, with warm interior bus light and soft natural surroundings.

**Ready prompt**

> A quiet photographic scene at a modern Dubai bus stop just after sunset. A real-looking full-size low-floor city bus has arrived accurately alongside the kerb in the lower half of the portrait frame, with correct wheel placement, realistic side mirrors and one plausible right-side boarding door. Neutral ivory bodywork, dark glass and a restrained warm interior light; no logos or operator branding. A simple steel-and-glass shelter and clean stone pavement define the boarding area. The image should feel like an ordinary service running smoothly. Use a natural 50 mm lens at passenger eye level, straight architectural lines, subtle warm-cool balance, believable dusk exposure and premium editorial detail. Leave the upper 40 percent as calm sky and unmarked glass surfaces for a later headline; keep the important bus and shelter details within the central 4:5 crop. No readable lettering, signs, plates, timetables, route displays or government emblems. No identifiable faces; keep the stop empty or any person tiny, rear-facing and unidentifiable. No traffic drama, siren lights, neon, purple, lens flare, glowing lines, holograms, robots, AI clichés, advertising graphics or watermark. Portrait 9:16. No text rendered into the image.

**Why it belongs:** The outcome is experienced as an uneventful arrival. That is the emotional meaning of calm flow restored.

## Selection and finishing

Choose a frame only when the vehicle, shelter and road geometry survive close inspection. Reject invented wheels, mirrored doors, broken handrails, fused road lanes and impossible reflections. Correct or regenerate malformed physical details before layout. Do not use painted-over text artifacts as decorative texture.

Keep natural material colour. Avoid heavy teal-and-orange grading or gold overlays. Headlines belong in reserved negative space; if legibility requires a dark scrim or a large gradient, choose another frame. Use an opaque Ivory panel only when the composition was deliberately planned for it.

Preserve photographic crop discipline across a deck: one dominant subject, one quiet text region, and no competing visual event. Store the final prompt, model, date and any reference-image permissions with the approved image. Use a real approved photograph whenever an actual vehicle specification, location or facility must be shown accurately.
