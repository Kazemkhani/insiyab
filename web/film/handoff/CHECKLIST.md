## RTA operations reviewer

Can a planner say which route, direction and hour to inspect, why, and what action remains theirs? Is spare availability reported rather than assumed? Is driver communication a clearly separate student drill rather than a claimed integration with RTA's actual dispatch process? Are the passenger consequences stated without claiming measured waiting counts or financial savings?

## Dataiku reviewer

Can every figure be traced to a named table/recipe and service day? Are measured planning outputs distinct from model outputs? Are split, horizon and denominator explicit? Is the simple rule visible? Does the strict-next-hour comparison honestly show simpler methods winning? Can the Flow and live notes be followed without relying on the film?

## Business development reviewer

Who adopts this, at which planning cycle, and what changes in their work? The immediate product is a reviewable route-hour worklist. A pilot should evaluate additional days, seasonal variation, service constraints, planner adoption and out-of-sample errors. Do not manufacture ROI, promise automatic dispatch, or imply an awarded government contract. The agentic-AI policy is context, not evidence of product value.

## Non-technical first-time viewer

After five seconds, what is this screen about? After the film, explain the problem and solution in your own words. What was measured, what was simulated, and who decides? If those answers are unclear, rewrite the offending sentence or shot. Do not add more charts to compensate.
