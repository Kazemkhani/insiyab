  3. InsightsFind the routes and hours to investigate.
  4. ForecastEstimate trip time and test the miss.
  5. DecisionsGive a planner evidence to review.

The planner's list and control-room flags come from measured trips. They do not consume the forecast. The six steps explain the work; they are not six literal Dataiku recipes.

Before the team walks in

  1. Each person explains their topic in thirty seconds without reading.
  2. Run the ten-minute timer once and rehearse the screen changes.
  3. Read the current number and its test window from the screen. Do not mix INSIYAB and John results.
  4. Keep the offline pack and public pitch open. Keep private call keys off the projector.
  5. Do the full phone rehearsal separately. Success means reports, a recorded human choice and all acknowledgements, not merely ringing.
  6. Use the illustrated fallback if the call loop or recording is not ready. Do not present playback as live.

If a judge challenges the evidence

“That is a fair limit. Here is the test we ran, what it shows, and the next test we would need.” Then open the source. Never fill a missing fact with a confident guess.
