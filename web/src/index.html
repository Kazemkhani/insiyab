How these errors are counted

MAE is the average absolute miss. RMSE gives larger misses more weight. MAPE is the average percentage miss on eligible bins. WAPE divides total absolute miss by total actual boardings. A dash means the bundle does not supply that value.

Sources and limits

The paths, stops and communities come from network.json. Buses follow replay/ stop times, interpolated along the supplied path between stops. The upright bars count boardings in the current quarter hour, not people currently waiting. Standing capacity is not verified.

Only one service day is supplied. A forecast tested on later hours of that day is not evidence about another day. A planner decides; nothing here changes a timetable or instructs a driver.
