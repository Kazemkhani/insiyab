# Lessons for Insiyab

Research checked 16 November 2026. These are outside examples, not evidence that Insiyab has changed transport in Dubai. The product continues to show invented data. Research used the agent-reach skill with Exa search and direct primary-source pages.

| Place | What the source supports | What to apply here |
|---|---|---|
| Singapore | LTA reported about 8% of North East Line commuters moving out of the morning peak after a combined programme of free off-peak rail travel and enhanced Travel Smart Journeys. It also expanded alternatives. This is a reported programme change, not an isolated causal estimate of rewards. | Put an actual alternative, departure time and journey penalty beside an offer. Do not insert 8% into our simulation. [LTA, 4 March 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/3/news-releases/8-percent-nel-commuters-shift-out-morning-peak-travel.html). |
| United States, San Francisco | The preliminary BART Perks report describes roughly 250 daily shifts among about 2,600 participating peak commuters, approximately 10%. It explicitly says the pilot was too small to produce noticeable system crowding reduction. | Keep participant response, network benefit and a room demonstration separate. Fixed-shift workers may not have the freedom to change time. [SFCTA, 8 March 2017, pp. 1–2](https://www.sfcta.org/sites/default/files/2019-04/SFCTA_Press_release_Incentives_Shift_BART_Riders_Out_of_the_Morning_Rush_03.08.2017.pdf). |
| United Kingdom, London | TfL evaluates frequent buses through regularity and excess waiting time; less frequent buses through timetable adherence. It uses arrival and departure records throughout the route. | Inspect a specific bus and stop. Keep lateness distinct from passenger waiting. Our boardings do not reveal a queue, and we cannot claim waiting-time savings without a validated method. [TfL quality of service indicators](https://tfl.gov.uk/forms/14144.aspx). |
| Finland, Helsinki | HSL's Kutsuplus report says the service worked technically and had high satisfaction, but its 15-vehicle scale required heavy subsidies. The service ceased at the end of 2015. | Treat on-demand transport as a bounded proposal. Do not infer financial viability from spare seats or a pleasing simulation. [HSL final report, May 2016, abstract and finances](https://www.hsl.fi/sites/default/files/uploads/8_2016_kutsuplus_finalreport_english.pdf). |
| Australia, New South Wales | The transport agency's on-demand pilot briefing establishes an experimental service programme rather than a universal replacement for scheduled buses. | Define a limited corridor, evaluation period and stop conditions before proposing expansion. [Transport for NSW industry briefing, December 2016](https://www.transport.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/media/documents/2017/on-demand-industry-briefing-14-dec-2016.pdf). |
| Hong Kong, China | MTR's Early Bird Discount publishes a precise exit window, designated stations and eligible payment product. The page describes eligibility; it does not establish a crowding effect. | Make reward eligibility understandable before asking for a decision. An attractive percentage alone says nothing about passenger benefit. [MTR Early Bird Discount](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/customer/main/early_bird.html). |
| Colombia, Bogotá | The mobility authority, World Bank and TransMilenio analysed changes in travel patterns across 2019, 2023 and 2025 using multiple data sources. | Make the one-day limitation visible. Request testing across days before claiming generalisation. Do not import mobile-network data into our privacy boundary. [Bogotá mobility observatory](https://observatorio.movilidadbogota.gov.co/publicaciones/informes/analisis-de-la-movilidad-con). |

## Product decisions

The strongest opportunity is a verifiable chain: a passenger has no seat, the forecast identifies where that will recur, a planner sees a feasible exchange or voluntary alternative, and the measured outcome is checked later. A generic live map alone does not establish that chain.

Implemented in this review: route focus; a selected bus's passengers against seats; a comparison against the lowest-error naive forecast at the displayed horizon; explicit route-series choice in the stop challenge; an inside/outside interval result; and exact supplied trip pairs in printed proposals. The renderer uses actual paths and stop times. Community names no longer obscure the network overview.

Keep incentives voluntary. Site workers with fixed start times and passengers who cannot walk should not become the assumed source of capacity. The planner's printed exchange explicitly requires review of depot, vehicle blocks, access and duties. These are constraints to validate, not claims that the present data proves feasibility.

Deferred to the owning lanes: service-level waiting-time measures; checked model-selection and uncertainty metadata; room authentication and the live depot reply; passenger-page walking and timing eligibility. No external uptake rate, cost saving or invented journey result is written into the bundle or the interface.
