## The three cheapest changes that add the most points

Each change can be completed before the Friday presentation with no new engineering.

**1. Add data.dubai average speed as a named feature (adds up to +3 points, mostly criterion 3.2)**

The judges named data.dubai average speed by route as the right congestion proxy (RTA statement, 18 November). It is publicly available and does not require a data agreement. Even if the speed table is added to the Dataiku flow as a lookup and noted in the MODEL-CARD.md rather than used as a live model feature, the act of naming it, showing where it would slot in, and giving its URL moves 3.2 from "Missing" to "Planned" and strengthens the pitch answer to "can you forecast another day?". A one-hour task on the data.dubai portal.

**2. Complete one clean phone co-pilot run before Friday, or document the 08:42 incomplete run clearly on screen (adds up to +2 points, criterion 1.2 and 2.3)**

The 08:42 run on 18 November reached four connected calls and recorded a manager decision but returned incomplete because one driver call went to voicemail and one acknowledgement was not confirmed. If a clean run is not feasible before Friday, labelling the recorded result clearly ("incomplete: one missed acknowledgement") and walking through what full completion looks like is stronger than avoiding it. Judges said agentic AI is acceptable provided the decision stays human (RTA statement). Demonstrating that the system shows "incomplete" rather than falsely claiming success directly earns points on 1.2 (actionable) and 2.3 (confident answers).

**3. Rehearse the seven-minute script end to end once, with timing (adds +1 to 2 points, criterion 2.3)**

Criterion 2.3 is delivery and Q&A: 6 points. The script is written. The Q&A cards are in this document and in section 13 of the continuation file. One full rehearsal with someone timing it and asking at least three of the five listed questions converts "Planned" to "Shown" for 2.3 at no cost beyond one hour of the team's time before Friday morning.
