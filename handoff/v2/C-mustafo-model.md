## Tasks, in order
1. **Features** (each must be computable at forecast time, with no look-ahead):
   - heat index from `opendata` hourly temperature and humidity (Steadman approximation; say December 2025 was mild, so its gain is expected small);
   - school-holiday and public-holiday flags (already in the calendar data);
   - the same vehicle's lateness at the end of its previous trip (the "bus still out" signal, as a number);
   - headway deviation at the stop (gap to the bus in front versus the timetable gap).
   Retrain, keep the chosen model family (boosted trees with a count loss for boardings; the lateness model as now) and re-run the full evaluation.
2. **Justify the choice** in the card: boosted trees vs the penalised Poisson model vs naive rules; one paragraph, with the numbers.
3. **Tables the judges should see** (the card generates them): lateness by horizon (1, 5, 10, 20 stops), by peak (16:00 to 20:00), by route group and community (cross-validation), with ranges; boardings by horizon with the honest "range includes zero" and the 120-minute loss kept in.
4. **`artifacts/forecast/judge_pick.json`** per `docs/v2/CONTRACT-V2.md`: for each route and hour 16:00 to 20:00 on the held-out part, forecast lateness, naive, actual, `n`. Aggregates only.
5. **`artifacts/forecast/scatter.json`**: per held-out evening trip, forecast error and timetable error; the share where the forecast was closer, computed.
6. One chart for the deck: lateness MAE by horizon, model vs best naive, with the range band (PNG and SVG into `artifacts/forecast/`; brand colours: ink for the model, ink 50% for naive, no rainbow).
